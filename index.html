Privacy Policy

What Dinner Party does with your information. The short answer: nothing. It stays on your phone.

Effective November 7, 2026

Dinner Party does not collect your information. There is no account to create, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

The recipes you save and your settings are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your phone

To work, the app keeps a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • The recipes you save or add from the catalog.
  • Your settings, such as how amounts are written and whether you prefer light or dark.
  • A note that you have already seen the welcome screens.

None of this leaves your phone. We have no server that could receive it.

Pictures of recipes

You can add a recipe by taking a photo or choosing a picture, such as a cookbook page or a screenshot. The app asks for camera or photo access only when you tap one of those buttons, and you can say no and still type or paste your recipe instead.

The text in the picture is read on your phone, using the text recognition built into your device. The picture is not uploaded anywhere, and the app does not keep it. Only the ingredient text you choose to save is stored.

Third parties

Dinner Party does not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services, and it does not share information with anyone. The app works without an internet connection.

If a paid option is added in the future, the purchase is handled by Apple. We do not see or store your payment details.

Phone backups

Your phone may include the app's data in its own backups, for example iCloud Backup, depending on your device settings. That is handled by Apple and by you, not by us.

Deleting your data

You are in control of everything the app stores. You can delete a single recipe from its Edit screen, delete all recipes from Settings, or delete the app, which removes all of its data from your phone.

Children

Dinner Party collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change with it. We will not start collecting information without updating this page first.
